# ✨ SellCraft AI

### Turn Product Details into Powerful Listings

SellCraft AI is a simple, browser-based product listing assistant designed for small online sellers, freelancers, resellers, and small businesses. It helps transform basic product information into structured, editable product listings for online marketplaces and social media.

The project is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a focus on simplicity, accessibility, and a clean user experience.

## 🚀 Features

- **Product Listing Generator:** Generates product titles and descriptions from user-provided details.
- **Key Features:** Organizes product information into readable bullet points.
- **Search Keywords:** Suggests relevant keywords based on product details.
- **Social Media Captions:** Creates captions for platforms such as Instagram and WhatsApp.
- **Custom Writing Tone:** Choose from Friendly, Professional, Playful, Minimal, and Premium.
- **Multiple Platforms:** Prepare content for websites, marketplaces, Instagram, and WhatsApp.
- **Editable Results:** Review and modify generated content before publishing.
- **Copy and Download:** Copy listing content or download it as a `.txt` file.
- **Saved Drafts:** Save, load, and delete listings using browser local storage.
- **Dark Mode:** Switch between light and dark themes.
- **Responsive Design:** Works on desktop and mobile screens.

## 💻 Getting Started

No installation or build tools are required for the current prototype.

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.
4. Enter your product name and product details.
5. Choose your preferred tone, platform, and language.
6. Click **Generate Listing**.
7. Edit, copy, download, or save your results.

## 🌱 Why I Built This

Writing product descriptions repeatedly can take time, especially for small sellers who manage their own product catalogues and social media pages.

SellCraft AI explores how a lightweight tool can make product content creation easier, organize information, and help sellers prepare listings more efficiently.

The goal is to keep the workflow simple and accessible without requiring users to learn complicated software.

## 🔓 Open Innovation and AI Roadmap

The current version uses JavaScript templates to generate listing content. It does **not yet integrate a real AI model**.

The next stage is to integrate an open-weight language model that can run locally on a user's computer.

Planned improvements include:

- Integration with a locally running open-weight model
- Better product-specific descriptions and keyword suggestions
- Support for more languages
- Reusable brand voice and writing preferences
- Batch listing generation
- Optional product image understanding

### Why Open-Source AI?

Integrating an appropriately licensed open-weight model could give users more control over their data and make it possible to generate content locally without sending every request to an external AI API.

It would also allow experimentation with different models and writing styles without depending entirely on one provider.

These benefits will depend on the model, its license, hardware requirements, and the eventual implementation.

## ⚠️ Current Limitations

- Listing generation currently uses templates rather than a real AI model.
- Generated content may require manual editing.
- Saved drafts are stored only in the current browser on the current device.
- The application does not directly publish listings to online marketplaces.
- No user accounts or cloud synchronization are implemented.

Always verify product specifications, pricing, materials, and other claims before publishing a listing.
